## Name

Cozy Treasure Hunter

## Short description (max 300 characters)

A quiet first-person treasure hunt on a sunny beach. Sweep the sand, dig up forgotten things, clean them by hand, dive for sunken gold and make a camp that feels like home. Misty mornings. Buried treasure. Nothing to rush.

## About this game (paste into the Steam description box; it uses Steam's markup)

```
[h2]A treasure hunt with nowhere to be[/h2]
Cozy Treasure Hunter is a gentle first-person game about a long beach, a metal detector and a very good dog. Sweep the sand until the detector sings, dig carefully, then clean what you find by hand until it shines. Sell it, recycle it, return it to the person who lost it, or keep it for the shelf at your camp.

[h2]Dig, clean, return[/h2]
[list]
[*] Sweep the sand with a detector that beeps faster the closer you get, then dig with your trowel.
[*] Scrub every find clean with a brush: over 40 different things, from rusty nails and soda tabs to pocket watches, rings, doubloons and two antiques you can piece back together.
[*] Return lost items to their owners and get letters back at your camp's post box.
[/list]

[h2]A camp that is yours[/h2]
[list]
[*] A campfire, a workbench, a radio with six stations, a recycling bin, a hammock to sleep until morning and a golf cart to drive.
[*] Furnish it: a woven rug, a painted sign you can rewrite, string lights, tiki torches, a beach umbrella, potted palms, a surfboard rack and a cooler.
[*] Look after a golden retriever who sniffs out finds, loves a good scratch and leaves paw prints in the sand.
[/list]

[h2]Beyond the beach[/h2]
[list]
[*] Take the boat out to a small island with its own finds, an old altar and a map to chests buried back home.
[*] Buy diving gear and swim down to a shipwreck. Spear fish, and watch turtles, octopuses, seahorses, sea snakes and crabs go about their day.
[*] Find a message in a bottle and follow its map to a hidden hatch under the sand.
[/list]

[h2]Under the dunes[/h2]
A stone room lit by torches, a captain's journal left open on a lectern and a great door that has been waiting a very long time for the right two antiques and the noon sun. What is behind it is yours to find, and the beach is still yours afterwards.

[h2]A world that changes around you[/h2]
[list]
[*] A full day and night cycle, with sunsets, stars and the occasional rainy day.
[*] Sharks, gulls, crabs and fish that go about their lives: shoot them with a toy pistol or a bow if you like, or just watch.
[*] Everything is built to be calm: no timers, no fail states, no one telling you what to do next.
[/list]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] Gamepad support, with on-screen prompts that follow what you are using
[*] Autosave, plus as many manual saves as you like
[*] 35 Steam achievements
[*] Steam Cloud saves
[*] Windowed, borderless and fullscreen, with an adjustable resolution
[/list]
```
